# Product

## What it is
A pipeline that turns one request into a real small or medium business app: online shops, Uzum-style marketplaces,
service and booking websites, team tools and personal apps. Each product gets a public website, a mobile app and a
staff area.

## Who uses what it produces
- **Shoppers and customers in Uzbekistan** (Tashkent and regions), on phones first, usually arriving from Telegram,
  Instagram or a shared link. They read Uzbek first, often Russian. They expect prices in so'm, cash on delivery or
  Click/Payme, phone-number contact, and to see what is in stock before committing.
- **Small business owners and their staff** (a florist, a jeweller, a salon, a clothes seller, a marketplace seller)
  who are not designers and maintain their own catalog from a phone.
- **Builders**, from beginners to developers, who choose words in a spec and expect a professional result.

## Brand commitments
- Every generated business must look like its own company. A jeweller and a marketplace must not look related.
- The result must never read as a template or a default admin kit.
- It must feel at home to local shoppers, not imported: familiar buying patterns from Uzum, Telegram and Instagram.
- Uzbek is the default language, with Russian and English available.

## Tone
Direct, warm and trustworthy. Plain words, real prices, no invented claims.

## Non-negotiables
Fast on mid-range phones and slow connections. Price, availability and the buy action are always easy to find.
